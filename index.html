How a project runs

From first message to a live site in four steps.

Step 1

Brief

You send your business details, logo, photos and what you want visitors to do. I ask any questions that are missing.

Step 2

Preview

I build a working first version and send you a link to look at on your own phone and computer.

Step 3

Revise

You tell me what to change. I update the text, colors and layout until you are happy with it.

Step 4

Launch

I help you publish the site on your domain and send you every file, with a short guide for small edits.

Questions

Before you write to me.

What do I need to send you?

Your business name, what you offer, contact details, and any logo or photos you have. If you do not have photos, I can suggest a layout that works without them.

Do you work with clients in other countries?

Yes. I work fully remotely and communicate in writing, so time zones are not a problem. I reply within 24 hours.

How do payments work?

I ask for half upfront and half on delivery. You see the working preview before you make the final payment.

Can you change the site later?

Yes. Small updates can be done as a separate request, and you always receive the source files, so you are never locked in.

Tell me about your project.

Send a short message with what your business does and what you want the site to achieve. I will reply with a clear scope, a price and a delivery date.
